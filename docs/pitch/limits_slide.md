# Stated limits

- Heuristic, no optimality guarantee; measured gap quoted on small instances
- One depot, one capacity dimension, no split parcels
- Closures modeled by departure time on a direct edge; no mid-edge rerouting, no automatic detours
- Vehicles in transit are committed to their next stop
- Windows are hard
- Target scale: up to 200 deliveries and 15 vehicles in about 2-3 s
